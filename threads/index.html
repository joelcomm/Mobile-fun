How to Play

You are shown three cue words. Each one forms a compound word or common phrase with the same hidden word.

Example:

SUN MOON FLASH

Answer: LIGHT (sunlight, moonlight, flashlight)

You have 4 guesses. Each wrong guess costs 20 points. Hints reveal letters of the answer but cost 15 points each.
