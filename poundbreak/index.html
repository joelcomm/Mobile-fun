A 5-level Pup Town rescue

Somebody's been snatching pups off the streets and stashing them across five wild floors — a concrete kennel, a hedge maze, a crystal cavern, an abandoned theme park, and finally the Boss Bulldog's space mothership. Grab your water pistol, splash the catchers, free every pup, and bring down the boss at the top.

  • 🐕 Rescue every caged pup on each floor
  • 🔑 Pick up collar keys to open locked doors
  • 🚪 Reach the EXIT to advance to the next floor
  • 💦 Defeat the Boss Bulldog on Floor 5
Phone: joystick (bottom-left) to move, swipe anywhere else to turn, tap 💦 to fire.
Mac/PC: W/S forward & back, A/D strafe, trackpad or mouse to turn, click or SPACE to fire, arrow keys turn (alt). Doors open on contact. Press ESC to release the cursor.
Map: if you are having a hard time finding the pups, press Q on desktop or tap the LEVEL pill in the HUD on mobile to cheat and see the map.
