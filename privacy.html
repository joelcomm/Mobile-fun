ACUMEN

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2025

Overview

Acumen is a free brain games app offering daily puzzle games. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

The short version: Acumen does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We have no user accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Your game progress stays on your device and nowhere else.

Information We Collect

Acumen does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not require or offer user accounts or registration.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or any other personal identifiers.
  • We do not collect usage analytics or behavioral data.
  • We do not use advertising networks or display ads.
  • We do not include any third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not access your device's camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other device features.

Local Storage

Acumen uses your browser's localStorage to save your game progress, scores, and streaks. This data:

  • Is stored entirely on your device.
  • Is never transmitted to any server.
  • Is never shared with any third party.
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

LocalStorage is not a cookie and cannot be used to track you across websites.

Cookies

Acumen does not use cookies of any kind. We do not use session cookies, persistent cookies, or any other cookie-based tracking mechanism.

Third-Party Services

Acumen uses one external service:

  • Google Fonts — We load the Inter typeface from Google Fonts to display text in the app. When you load Acumen, your browser makes a request to Google's servers to fetch the font files. Google may log this request per their standard practices. You can review Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Apart from Google Fonts, Acumen makes no network requests to any third-party service. There are no analytics platforms, no ad networks, no social media integrations, and no other external dependencies that could collect your data.

Children's Privacy

Acumen is safe for users of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, there is no risk of collecting information from children. The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no social features, and no external links within the games themselves.

Parents and guardians can feel confident that Acumen fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide, as no personal data is collected from any user regardless of age.

Data Security

Since Acumen does not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no personal data at risk of breach or unauthorized access. Your game data exists only in your browser's localStorage on your own device and is subject to whatever security measures your device and browser provide.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, we will update this page and revise the effective date at the top. Since Acumen does not collect any contact information, we encourage you to review this page periodically. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting.

Our commitment to not collecting personal data is fundamental to Acumen. If this ever changes, we will make it clearly known before any data collection begins.
