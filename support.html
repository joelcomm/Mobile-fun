Frequently Asked Questions
What is Acumen? ›
Acumen is a free collection of daily brain games designed to challenge your memory, logic, vocabulary, and reflexes. Each day brings fresh puzzles across all games. There are no ads, no accounts, and no in-app purchases.
How do I play? ›
From the main screen, tap any game card to start playing. Each game has its own rules and instructions shown at the start. New puzzles are available every day at midnight. You can play all the games in any order.
I lost my progress. Can I get it back? ›
Game progress is stored locally in your browser's storage (localStorage). If you clear your browser data, use a different browser, or switch devices, your progress and streaks will be lost. Unfortunately, since no data is stored on any server, there is no way to recover lost progress. We recommend avoiding clearing site data for Acumen if you want to keep your streaks.
When do new puzzles appear? ›
New daily puzzles are available every day at midnight (based on your local time zone). You can check the countdown timer on the Stats page to see exactly when the next set of puzzles will be available.
Can I replay a puzzle I already completed today? ›
Once you complete a daily puzzle, your result is saved and you can view it, but you cannot replay the same puzzle until the next day's puzzle becomes available. This keeps the daily challenge fair and consistent for everyone.
How do streaks work? ›
Each game tracks your streak independently. Your streak increases by one for every consecutive day you complete that game. If you miss a day, your streak resets to zero. Check the Stats page to see all your current streaks at a glance.
Does Acumen work offline? ›
Acumen uses a service worker to cache game assets, so many of the games can work offline once they have been loaded at least once. However, the initial load and daily puzzle data may require an internet connection.
Is Acumen free? ›
Yes, Acumen is completely free. There are no ads, no premium tiers, no in-app purchases, and no subscriptions. All games are fully available to everyone.
Does Acumen collect my personal data? ›
No. Acumen does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. All game data is stored locally on your device. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
The app is not loading or looks broken. What should I do? ›
Try these steps:
  • Refresh the page (pull down on mobile, or press Ctrl+R / Cmd+R on desktop).
  • Make sure you have an active internet connection.
  • Try clearing the browser cache for this site only (not all site data, to preserve your progress).
  • Try a different browser to see if the issue persists.
  • If the problem continues, contact us at the email below.
